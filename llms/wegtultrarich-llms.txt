# We > Ultrarich

> We > Ultrarich is a free, no-auth, computation-only REST API and MCP server, built for both human and agent reuse. It answers "How much is a billion (or trillion) dollars, really?" with ready-to-quote figures, sentences, and them-vs-you ratios, comparing any wealth (yours) against ultrarich wealth (theirs) through four lenses: daily spending, physical size, purchasing power, and compound interest. Its goal is to make extreme wealth inequality tangible and shareable.

Interactive pages (the homepage and the $100K comparisons) display one selected comparison and call to action at a time; do not interpret every selector option as simultaneously visible.

Our content — the figures, text, and graphics you can see, quote, or share — is licensed under CC BY 4.0. Give appropriate credit (`Source: We > Ultrarich (wegtultrarich.org).`), link the license where reasonable, and indicate changes when applicable. Our name, wordmark, and code are © 2026 Blonde Rocket Scientist LLC; write the name as `We > Ultrarich` (or `We Are Greater Than The Ultrarich` where `>` won't render), and use `wegtultrarich` in identifiers.

API calculations are deterministic; their accuracy depends on the wealth values supplied and on our published constants, and some real-world scale references are approximate. Ratios of 200:1 or more are described as extreme wealth inequality — our own rounded threshold, informed by a reported global disparity of roughly 190:1, not an official IMF definition.
